## The client

Azienda agricola Cavagliato has grown peppers in Carmagnola, near Turin, since 1926: four generations of one family. It belongs to the Association of Corno di Carmagnola Pepper Producers, the only edition entitled to the **Slow Food Presidium** label.

The website had to tell this story and introduce the products to people who buy directly from the farm, without losing the warmth of a family business.

## The project

A multi-page website with a warm, recognizable identity: pepper red, calligraphic headings and plenty of photos of the crops.

- **Home**: a full-screen hero with the logo, the farm's four strengths, the associations it belongs to and a photo gallery with a lightbox.
- **Products**: a card for each of the 8 varieties, from the four native Carmagnola varieties to the hot chili peppers, with a badge for the type, a description and serving tips.
- **Recipes**: 8 recipes with times and difficulty, from classic peperonata to "Filetti alla Cavagliato".
- **History and awards**: the family's vintage photos and the awards received.
- **Contact**: an email form handled in PHP, contact details and a Google map to find the farm.

## The details

- Designed mobile-first, with a "Contact us" button always within thumb's reach.
- Light animations as sections appear, and a navbar that stays fixed while scrolling.
- A simple, lightweight stack that's easy to maintain over time, for a client who wants a website that just works.

## The result

The website did its job: since it went live, **sales to private customers have grown strongly**, with orders arriving **from abroad too**. The Corno di Carmagnola now reaches people who would never have heard of the farm, and the always-visible "Contact us" button shortens the path from curiosity to order.
